# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive community in which people can learn, contribute, and exchange technical ideas regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with respect and consideration.
- Give constructive feedback about ideas and work.
- Welcome questions and different perspectives.
- Respect privacy and consent.
- Accept corrections and take responsibility for the impact of your actions.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's private information without permission are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces. It also applies when someone represents the project publicly.

## Reporting

Report concerns to a repository maintainer. Maintainers can be identified through repository ownership and recent pull request activity. If no private contact method is listed on a maintainer's GitHub profile, request a private contact method in a repository issue without including sensitive details or identifying affected individuals.

Share only the information necessary to explain the concern. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and limit disclosure to what is needed to investigate and respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports and respond in proportion to the behavior and its impact. Actions may include requesting a correction, removing content, issuing a warning, or restricting participation temporarily or permanently. Maintainers involved in a report should defer its review to another maintainer where possible.
